# rclone (Suparig)

This is the home page for "rclone," a personal Google OAuth app registered by Suparig. It lets the owner use [rclone](https://rclone.org), an open-source command-line tool, to copy and manage files in their own Google Drive, such as Google Takeout archives.

The app is not offered as a service to the public.
